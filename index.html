Common questions

What Practices Ask Us

Straight answers about how our physician referral outreach works.

Is this a contract?

There is no contract; we're month to month. We do ask that you commit to at least six months to give it time to build results, and if you ever want to cancel, we just ask for thirty days' notice. If you're not seeing value, we don't want you sticking around out of obligation.

How is this different from a typical marketing agency?

We're not running ads or managing your social media. We're building real, face-to-face trust with the providers who send you patients. It's in-person outreach, not digital marketing.

Do you work with my specialty?

Yes, we work with specialist practices of any kind and connect you with the providers and other referring physicians most likely to send patients your way.

How soon will I start seeing results?

It takes time to build a network of providers who know and trust you, which is part of why we ask for that six-month commitment. You'll see activity and reporting from the start, but real growth builds over those first few months.

Is my territory still available?

Since we only represent one specialist per territory, availability depends on your specialty and area. Call us to check.

What exactly happens during an office visit?

We build and maintain connections with the providers that matter most to your practice. Happy to walk you through exactly how it works on a call.
