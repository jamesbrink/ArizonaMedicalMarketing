Website analytics and advertising

Our marketing pages use Google Analytics to understand page visits and scrolling. Google processes website usage, browser and device information, and approximate location information, and uses cookies to distinguish visits. Analytics starts automatically when you visit; this site does not display an analytics preference popup.

Our Analytics configuration removes query strings and fragments from page URLs, limits referrers to their website origin, and does not collect contact-form contents or contact-link destinations. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled for this Analytics destination.

We also use an existing Google Ads tag to measure advertising performance, including a conversion after a successful contact-form submission. This advertising setup operates separately from our Analytics reporting.

You can manage cookies in your browser and learn more about how Google uses information from partner sites or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Contact requests

When you submit our contact form, Formspree processes your name, email address, and message so we can respond. Please do not include patient information or sensitive medical details. See Formspree's privacy policy.
